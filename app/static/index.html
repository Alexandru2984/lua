LuaLens. Small language. Wider perspective.

A clearer view, in a few checks.

Enter a domain or public IPv4 address. You can paste an HTTP(S) URL; LuaLens analyzes its hostname on the standard web ports.

◈

Quick look

A, AAAA, MX, NS, TXT, CAA and DMARC records, verified HTTPS, seven header checks, robots.txt, sitemap.xml and security.txt.

◎

Deep dive

Adds historical hostnames from certificate transparency and registration dates/status from RDAP. Providers can time out; unavailable checks are shown explicitly.

▤

Keep the useful parts

Favorite reports, compare two reports for the same domain, copy records, and export JSON or a spreadsheet-friendly CSV.

◇

Your private workspace

A secure cookie identifies this browser's workspace for 30 days. Clearing it loses access to its reports. Reports expire after 30 days; the service also keeps at most 5,000 finished reports globally. Favorites do not extend retention. Deleted reports may remain in private backups for up to 14 days. There are no accounts or automatic scheduled scans.

Up to three new scans per minute per client, one active scan per workspace and three across the service. Recent results can be reused for ten minutes. DNS lookups and provider queries disclose the target to those providers. IPv6-only destinations are currently unsupported. This dashboard remains authenticated and excluded from search indexing.
